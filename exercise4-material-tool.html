Manufacturing Process of Aluminum Alloy Windows

I. Three Main Material Categories for Window Components

Metal Materials (Aluminum Alloy Profiles): Window frames, sashes, mullions, and pressure lines (edge strips for fixing glass). Typically uses 6063-T5 aluminum alloy, known for high strength, corrosion resistance, and lightweight properties.

Polymer Materials (PVC/PA66/EPDM Rubber):

  • PA66 GF25 (Nylon + Glass Fiber): Thermal break strips used in thermal break aluminum structures to block heat transfer.
  • PVC/ABS: Corner keys, rollers, and outer casings for window handles.
  • EPDM Rubber (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer): Sealing strips for sealing between glass and profiles, and between sashes and frames.

Inorganic Non-metallic Materials (Float Glass/Tempered Glass): Glass panels (typically double or triple insulating tempered glass) that provide lighting, sound insulation, and thermal insulation.
